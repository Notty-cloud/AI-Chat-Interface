ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
AI Chat Interface — Language Translation Service
Version 1.0 | Effective: January 2026

================================================================
1. PURPOSE
================================================================
This policy defines acceptable and prohibited uses of the AI Chat
Interface translation and document Q&A service. All users are
required to comply with this policy when using the platform.

================================================================
2. PERMITTED USES
================================================================

2.1 Personal Translation
Users may translate personal documents, messages, images, and
web content for their own understanding and use.

2.2 Academic and Research Use
Translation of academic papers, research materials, and
educational content for study or research purposes is permitted.

2.3 Business Communications
Translation of business correspondence, contracts, product
information, and internal documents for professional use is
permitted, provided the user has the right to process the content.

2.4 Document Indexing (RAG)
Users may upload documents to the RAG pipeline for Q&A purposes.
Uploaded documents are stored in memory only for the duration of
the session and are not retained after the session ends.

2.5 News and Public Information
Translation of publicly available news articles, government
announcements, and publicly accessible web content is permitted.

================================================================
3. PROHIBITED USES
================================================================

3.1 Hate Speech and Harmful Content
The service must not be used to translate, generate, or distribute
content that:
- Incites violence or hatred against individuals or groups
- Promotes discrimination based on race, religion, gender,
  nationality, disability, or sexual orientation
- Constitutes harassment or targeted abuse

3.2 Confidential Third-Party Content
Users must not upload or submit content that:
- Belongs to a third party without their explicit consent
- Is subject to a non-disclosure agreement
- Contains trade secrets or proprietary business information
  of another organisation

3.3 Personal Data
Users should not submit content containing sensitive personal
data of third parties (e.g. medical records, financial statements,
identity documents) unless they have lawful authority to process
that data and take full responsibility for compliance with
applicable data protection laws.

3.4 Illegal Content
Translation or processing of content that is illegal in the user's
jurisdiction is strictly prohibited, including but not limited to:
- Content that infringes intellectual property rights
- Material that facilitates fraud or financial crime
- Content prohibited under applicable export control regulations

================================================================
4. DATA HANDLING
================================================================

4.1 Session Storage
All uploaded documents and chat history are stored in memory
only for the active browser session. No data is persisted to
a database or retained after the session ends.

4.2 API Processing
Messages and images are sent to the OpenAI API for processing.
Users should review OpenAI's data usage policies at
platform.openai.com/docs/policies before submitting sensitive
content.

4.3 No Training Use
This application does not use submitted content to train or
fine-tune any AI model.

================================================================
5. ACCURACY DISCLAIMER
================================================================

5.1 Translation Accuracy
AI-generated translations are provided for informational and
general use. They should not be relied upon as the sole basis
for legal, medical, financial, or safety-critical decisions
without review by a qualified human translator.

5.2 Image Extraction
Text extraction from images is subject to image quality and
script complexity. Results may be incomplete for very low-quality,
handwritten, or highly stylised text.

5.3 No Liability
The platform operators accept no liability for decisions made
on the basis of AI-generated translations or document summaries.

================================================================
6. ENFORCEMENT
================================================================
Violation of this policy may result in suspension of access to
the service. Suspected illegal activity will be reported to the
relevant authorities.

For questions about this policy, contact the platform administrator.
