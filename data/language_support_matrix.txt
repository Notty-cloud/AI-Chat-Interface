KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================

1. Handwriting
   Handwritten text in images is supported but accuracy depends
   heavily on legibility. Print handwriting is better than cursive.
   Estimated accuracy: 70-85% for clear handwriting.

2. Archaic or Classical Languages
   Old English, Latin, Classical Arabic, Classical Chinese, and
   Ancient Greek can be translated but confidence is reduced.
   Always validate with a subject matter expert.

3. Technical and Domain-Specific Jargon
   Medical, legal, and engineering terminology may be translated
   literally rather than using domain-standard equivalents.
   Flag these translations for expert review.

4. Dialects and Regional Variants
   The model may not reliably distinguish between regional dialects
   (e.g. Moroccan Darija vs. Egyptian Arabic, Rioplatense Spanish
   vs. Mexican Spanish). The output will note uncertainty.

5. Mixed-Script Documents
   Documents mixing multiple scripts (e.g. Japanese with English
   loanwords in Katakana) are handled correctly by the vision
   model but may require post-processing for formatting.
